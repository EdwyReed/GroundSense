GroundSense - it is a geospatial platform that helps assess whether unpaved and forest roads are likely to be passable for a specific vehicle. It combines open satellite imagery, weather data, soil properties, and terrain analysis to map the risk of getting stuck, forecast deterioration after rain or snowmelt, and estimate when conditions may improve. Through an interactive map, users can specify vehicle weight and configuration, explore road conditions across a region, and make better-informed transport decisions, with uncertainty clearly shown rather than a guarantee of safe passage.
